What's included

Eight ways we support your IPO.

01

Equity Story & Investment Highlights

Optimally position the issuer and formulate the equity story, based on industry intelligence and market interaction.

02

Quantitative Analysis

Undertake internal quantitative analysis to pre-empt market reaction and analysts' queries.

03

IPO Collaterals

Factsheet, management presentation slides, media kits and investment merits to attract the investment community.

04

Training & Dry Runs

Guidance on collaterals, main messages and roadshow conduct for IPO marketing.

05

Press & Media Releases

Disseminate qualitative information and generate maximum public impact to support the IPO.

06

Ceremonies

Prospectus launch and listing ceremonies, including coordinating invitations to media and analysts, gambit planning and event protocols.

07

Media Monitoring

Related industry news, company news and press monitoring updates via email, where applicable.

08

Post-IPO Retainer

Post-IPO support on a retainer basis to build on the momentum created during the IPO stage.
